About the project

Mohnish Bangari | Equity Research

A completed independent equity-research project built to understand how different businesses create value, how valuation changes the investment question, and how a research portfolio performed over a fixed historical window.

What the project includes

  • Eight company research reports
  • Company-specific operating metrics
  • Competitive advantages, risks and growth drivers
  • Valuation discussion and scenario analysis
  • A $100,000 equal-weight historical model portfolio
  • S&P 500 benchmark comparison
  • A completed performance review
  • Source links for financial and market data

Why these companies?

The group intentionally mixes business models: Apple, Microsoft and NVIDIA represent technology and AI; Amazon combines commerce, cloud and advertising; Visa and JPMorgan show two very different financial models; Costco represents membership retail; and Coca-Cola provides a consumer-staples comparison.
