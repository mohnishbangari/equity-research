Study period

July 20 → September 25, 2026

The portfolio begins with $12,500 in each company using July 20 closing prices and holds the positions unchanged through September 25. The final reports use information available by the end of the period, so this is a retrospective research study rather than a claim that the final conclusions were known on day one.

Result

$106,499 ending value

The portfolio gained 6.50% versus 4.03% for the S&P 500, a difference of 2.47 percentage points. Microsoft was the largest contributor; Costco and Amazon were the only holdings with negative price returns.

Coverage

Different businesses require different evidence.

The same template is not forced onto every company. NVIDIA is judged on AI infrastructure growth and margins; JPMorgan on capital, credit and returns; Costco on membership economics; Visa on payment volume and network scale.

What the project tests

Business quality is not the same as stock performance.

Costco posted strong operating results yet had the weakest stock return in the study. Microsoft had the strongest return. The difference is a reminder that valuation and changing expectations matter alongside business performance.

Research standard

Primary sources first.

Financial claims come from company earnings releases, SEC filings and investor-relations materials. Historical prices are fixed to the study dates so the result cannot be changed after the fact.
