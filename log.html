Performance review

What the completed period actually showed.

The portfolio beat the S&P 500 during the selected window, but the more useful result is understanding where the gains came from, where the thesis and price diverged, and what the short period cannot prove.

1. The result was concentrated.

Microsoft added about $3,538 and NVIDIA added about $1,340. Together they generated roughly 75.1% of the portfolio's total dollar gain. The portfolio outperformed, but that outperformance depended heavily on two technology holdings.

2. Strong businesses can still lag.

Costco reported strong FY2026 sales, membership-fee and operating-income growth, yet its stock fell 1.39% during the period. This is the clearest case in the study showing why operating quality and valuation must be analyzed separately.

3. Capital intensity matters.

Amazon was nearly flat even though AWS and operating income grew strongly. Free cash flow was pressured by large AI infrastructure spending. The period reinforces the need to look beyond revenue and EPS when a company is entering a heavy investment cycle.

4. Diversification helped.

Coca-Cola gained 6.93%, contributing about $866. Its role was very different from Microsoft or NVIDIA: slower growth, defensive demand and brand pricing power. A diversified research set made the portfolio less dependent on one business model.

Completed outcome

Portfolio +6.50% vs. S&P 500 +4.03%

The difference was 2.47 percentage points. Because the window is only about two months, I would treat this as a documented outcome rather than evidence of a repeatable investing advantage. The strongest evidence in the project is the quality and consistency of the research process, not the short-term return.
